Privacy Policy – Popcorn Cascade

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Popcorn Cascade is an incremental popcorn game played in short timed runs: you heat kernels until they pop in chain reactions, spend your coins on upgrades between runs, and zoom out to ever bigger scales – from a frying pan to a field, the sky, a planet and outer space. Popcorn Cascade does not collect, store, or share any personal data directly. The game does not require user registration and does not collect personal information such as name, email address, or location.

However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information automatically to provide their services.

Advertising

Popcorn Cascade uses third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to display interstitial and rewarded ads. These services may collect certain data automatically, including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage data

This information is managed directly by the advertising provider and is subject to their respective privacy policies. You can review Google's advertising practices here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before any personalised advertising identifier is used. You can reset or delete that advertising identifier at any time from your device's Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Analytics

Popcorn Cascade uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics, such as game events and session information, that help us improve the game. This data is associated with a randomly generated identifier and does not include personal information such as name, email address, or precise location.

Google Play Services

The app may use Google Play Services, which may collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. For more information, please review:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchase

Popcorn Cascade offers an optional one-time purchase that removes ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive or store your payment details. The game only keeps on the device the fact that the purchase was made.

Local Data

Game progress – coins, golden salt, upgrades, the skill tree, the current scale, best run and statistics – and settings are stored locally on your device, together with a diagnostic log of the current session – device model, screen size and language – that is kept on the device and used to investigate crashes.

Some of that progress travels with the anonymous analytics events described above, such as how many runs you have played, which scale you are in, how far you got in the tutorial and which ads you chose to watch. Your coins, golden salt and the rest of your progress never leave your device, and everything listed here is deleted when the app is uninstalled. There is no account, no cloud save and no server: the game works fully offline.

Children's Privacy

Popcorn Cascade is not specifically directed at children under 13 years of age. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and the Effective Date above will be updated.
